Faqs

Do you bring all the cooking equipment for live dosa catering?

We bring all the required equipment including cooker, gas, and other essentials.

Do you provide plates, spoons, and napkins?

Yes, we provide biodegradable disposable plates, spoons, forks, and napkins unless you specify otherwise.

How are the charges calculated for dosa catering?

We will go ahead with the plate count, not the head count. Please note only plate for one person

Is there a transportation fee for catering services?

Yes, transportation is charged at £2 per mile to and from the destination.

How long do you serve food at an event?

We serve food for 2 hours at your premises, with an additional 30 minutes required for setup. Extra time beyond this will be charged at £60 per hour.

What do customers need to provide for the setup?

Not required we will bring our own tables

What is the payment policy for booking live dosa catering?

30% token of advance to cofirm the event balance can we paid end of the day

Can I customize the menu with additional items?

Yes you can add with any extra cost

Do you provide tents or gazebos for outdoor catering?

No, we do not provide tents or gazebos.

Do your dishes contain allergens such as nuts or sesame?

At Arya Bhavan Restaurant, our food is prepared in a kitchen where nuts, sesame, and other allergenic ingredients are used, and despite our care, we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from allergen traces. Therefore, our menu may not be suitable for individuals with severe food allergies.
