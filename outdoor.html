Faqs

Do you cater South and North Indian vegetarian food?

Yes. Arya Bhavan offers outdoor catering with a full range of South Indian and North Indian pure vegetarian dishes for all events.

Which areas in London do you cover for catering services?

We cater across London, including Central London, Wembley, Tooting, Barking, Ilford, and East Ham.

Can you offer live dosa catering at events?

Absolutely. Our live dosa stations are one of our most popular catering options and can be added to any event package.

Do you provide vegan and Jain food options in your catering?

Yes, we offer dedicated vegan and Jain vegetarian dishes prepared separately to meet specific dietary preferences.

How far in advance should we book outdoor catering?

We recommend booking 1–2 weeks in advance, especially for weekends or large events, to ensure availability.

What types of events do you cater for?

We cater for weddings, birthdays, corporate events, religious functions, family gatherings, and outdoor community events.

Is all your catering food 100% vegetarian?

Yes. Arya Bhavan is a 100% pure vegetarian caterer, with no meat, fish, or eggs used in any dish.

Can you customise the catering menu for our event?

Yes, our team can fully customise South Indian and North Indian vegetarian menus based on your event theme and guest preferences.

Do you provide staff and equipment for outdoor catering?

Yes, we offer serving staff, buffet setups, live stations, and all essential catering equipment upon request
